R-YORS Life attribution and license
==================================

SRC/APPS/life.asm is an independent 16x16 W65C02 implementation of
Conway's Game of Life. John Horton Conway devised the rules in 1970;
Martin Gardner popularized them in the October 1970 Scientific American
"Mathematical Games" column. These credits identify the origin of the
rules, not authorship of this implementation or endorsement of R-YORS.
